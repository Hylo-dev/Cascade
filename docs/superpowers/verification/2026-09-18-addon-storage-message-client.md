## Contract and limits

The channel is trusted infrastructure already bound to the authenticated connection: it verifies physical generation/sequence, consumes the exact receipt and frees the staging before returning the response. The client verifies the bounded bytes and the public correlation; it does not authenticate evidence absent from those bytes. A response rejection after commit is not a rejection of the request: unresolved mutations stay outcomeUnknown and are not retried.

A slot holds the whole operation until finalization or drain. Closing and final delivery share the ordering under lock; cancellation before the handoff prevents sending, and after a possible handoff it preserves the uncertainty of the mutations. No lock crosses an await. External allocations, encoding and results must be admitted by the integration before they are built: the public client contains no allocator. The test's 8 MiB are a conservative choice of the fixture, not a Foundation/RSS measurement.

The finite matrix is not exhaustive: some combinations of close during preparation, drift during the response and cancellation of close callers are missing, as are SDK-specific asserts for maximum reason/versionConflict. The publication/asset attempts of the new fixture prove the adapter's entry guard; they are not presented as standalone evidence of the cross-family runtime credit. The end-to-end evidence for the storage path and the receipts remains valid.

The review's PARTIAL on the pending root steps is superseded by the evidence above, not by rewriting the historical report. Still open: native transport/bootstrap, production allocations/RSS, macOS 14/Intel, composition in the app, process qualification and complete C5/C12. `observeExit` in the fixture is a simulated input; C0d keeps the unconditional exit 78.
