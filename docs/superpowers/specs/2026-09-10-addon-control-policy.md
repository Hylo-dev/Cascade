# Native addons: accepted limit on delegated work

Date: 10 September 2026. User decision: "I'd say we take on the risk". It refers to the choice between direct process control and a guarantee extended to the work launched autonomously through macOS, after the risks were explained in the conversation.

## Approved guarantee

Work continues with the on-demand native system, host-retained content, SwiftUI and the shared services already designed. Cascade measures and governs the resources of the processes it manages and of the operations that go through its own services.

The current impossibility of guaranteeing containment, consumption attribution and stopping of all the work that an addon has macOS launch autonomously outside that path, for example through Launch Services, is accepted. That work can continue after the addon is disabled or Cascade quits. There is no promise that the disable button also stops such external work.

This change concerns that precise boundary. It grants no new macOS permissions and does not disable the sandbox of managed processes. It does not make acceptable an orphaned managed process, the use of an old identity or session, a duplicated command or quotas bypassed through Cascade's services. Work delegated through the broker stays accounted for and subject to grants.

The profile is a host choice, the same for team and external addons. It cannot be selected or widened with a declaration in the manifest. Signing serves to verify origin and integrity; it does not prove the addon's good behavior. A curated initial distribution is a recommendation for the release, not a technical exemption or a new mandatory restriction on the product.

## Requirements still binding

- Effective stop and verification of the exit of managed processes, including Cascade quitting/crashing and the death of the supervisor.
- Authenticated identity and a session revoked after an executable change; no termination of processes identified only by name or PID.
- Revalidated service permissions, application limits, finite queues and revocation; CPU/RAM observed with a declared interval and a declared possible temporary overshoot.
- No custom addon code in Cascade's graphics process, no individual polling and no process kept alive only to show ordinary content.
- Remote UI, publishers and operating systems qualified with their respective proofs before declared support.

## Consequences for the plan

The product decision is resolved and must not be requested again. The launcher is not yet qualified: what remains to be solved is above all the worker surviving the death of the supervisor and the identity after exec. Accepting a risk is not a new technical proof.

C0 continues only on those defects and on the qualification of the new profile. The old `launcher-admission` record and its negative results stay unchanged. Do not delete the counterexamples and do not turn `delegatedWorkControlled=false` into true.

For the next qualification, produce a distinct `launcher-admission-direct-v1` record, with `policyID: native-direct-control-v1`, `acceptedLimitations: [autonomousOSDelegation]` and mandatory checks set to true: `managedStop`, `hostExitCleanup`, `hostCrashCleanup`, `supervisorDeathStopsWorker`, `identitySafe`, `sessionInvalidatedAfterExec`, `cpuReadable`, `footprintReadable`. The producer and the admission check must verify the scenario, the policy and the exact limitation; skipped or unverified mandatory cases still prevent a PASS. The behavior of delegated work stays in the observations and in the characterization tests. These new records are not yet implemented nor generated by this documentation change.

Once those proofs pass, C1 is completed and work continues with runtime, Clock and timer, migrations and distribution according to the plan. No change to the language, to the SwiftUI mode or to the verification criteria of the other phases is implicitly authorized.

References: [completion plan](../plans/2026-09-10-addon-runtime-completion.md), [original proofs](../verification/2026-09-10-addon-launcher-decision.md).

## Emergency global recovery: decision of 24 September 2026

Decision `native-addon-global-recovery`, version 1, status **closed**.
To the proposal of allowing a restart of the whole of Cascade as a last resort after
selective recovery fails, the user answered: "accepted, continue".

A temporary interruption of healthy addons too is allowed when the control
infrastructure can no longer stop the affected chain. Selective recovery
remains the ordinary path; the global restart is the last resort, not the normal
disable behavior. Always-selective recovery is no longer an absolute
constraint for this emergency case.

Still mandatory: effective exit of managed processes, no orphans even
before main, incarnation identity, sandbox/signing, no addon code in the
GUI and qualification of external addons/operating systems. Launching a
new instance while the previous chain is in an unknown state is not approved, nor is ignoring
recovery errors or introducing an unbounded restart loop.

The [XPC proofs](../verification/2026-09-24-addon-xpc-frontier.md) motivate this choice,
but do not yet qualify the whole launcher. The gate stays unchanged until the
required proofs. This decision is distinct from the limitation on delegated work
and does not reopen the already rejected exception on orphaned managed processes.

The previous admission records stay historical. A global restart cannot be
recorded as proof of a selective stop while the host is alive: the future qualification
must identify this path and its recovery conditions separately.
