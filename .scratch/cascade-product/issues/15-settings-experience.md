## Question

How should the settings under the notch be organized, with the visual language of the macOS settings, so that appearance, per-display sizes, Live Activities and smart screens are manageable? Also prototype extension management, permissions for the chosen capabilities, shortcuts, restoring the defaults and accessibility. Distinguish global, per-display, per-app and per-instance preferences without inventing options not required by the previous decisions.
